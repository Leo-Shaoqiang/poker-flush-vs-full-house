Why Does a Full House Win?

In standard Texas Hold'em poker, hands are ranked purely by their mathematical probability. The rarer a hand combination is to achieve from a 52-card deck, the higher it ranks.

Full House (Boat) 🏆

Rank: #4 of 10

Combinations: 3,744

Probability: 0.1441% (1 in 694)

Example: K♠ K♦ K♣ 4♥ 4♠

Flush (Any Suit)

Rank: #5 of 10

Combinations: 5,108

Probability: 0.1965% (1 in 508)

Example: A♠ K♠ Q♠ J♠ 9♠ (Ace-High)
